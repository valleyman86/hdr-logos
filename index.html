I found HDR hiding in an ordinary webpage.

I was scrolling through job listings when one logo looked much brighter than everything around it. I made this comparison to understand why.

The files contain the same picture. Only the color profile differs. On an HDR-capable setup, the right image may appear noticeably brighter.

That is mildly interesting—and a little concerning. HDR could make logos, ads, or other art demand more attention than nearby SDR content.

If they look identical, your setup may not be showing the PQ image in extended dynamic range. Screenshots may also flatten the difference.
