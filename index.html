HDR images can exist on ordinary webpages.

On an HDR display, a logo or graphic can appear much brighter than the SDR content around it. This can pull attention in ways viewers may not expect.

If they look identical, your browser, display, or operating system may not be presenting the PQ image in extended dynamic range. Screenshots may also flatten the difference.
